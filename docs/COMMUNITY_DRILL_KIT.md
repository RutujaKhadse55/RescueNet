# RescueNet Community Disaster Preparedness Drill Kit (Phase 16)
## Turnkey 45-Minute Community Workshop & Mock Emergency Exercise

**Target Groups:** Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs), Village Panchayats, School Safety Committees, Industrial Workplaces  
**Equipment Needed:** Handsets with RescueNet installed, 1 whistle/megaphone, volunteer vests.

---

## 1. Workshop Agenda (45 Minutes Total)

```
[ 00:00 - 00:10 ]  Part 1: The Blackout Problem (Why cell towers fail during floods/quakes)
[ 00:10 - 00:20 ]  Part 2: App Setup & Bluetooth Verification (Setting up 5 languages & offline profile)
[ 00:20 - 00:35 ]  Part 3: The 15-Minute Mock Earthquake Drill (Simulating buried phones & homing)
[ 00:35 - 00:45 ]  Part 4: Debrief & Family Emergency Plan (Safety check and kit distribution)
```

---

## 2. Facilitator Script & Drill Activity

### Drill Activity: "Find the Buried Survivor"
1. **Divide participants into two groups:**
   - **Group A (Sheltering Victims):** 5 participants hide in classrooms, stairwells, or behind garden walls with their phones in **SOS mode**.
   - **Group B (Community Volunteers):** 5 participants act as search teams using **Rescuer Homing Mode**.
2. **Rules:**
   - Cellular data and Wi-Fi MUST be switched OFF on all phones.
   - Searchers look exclusively at the **`WARMER` / `COLDER`** signal trends and listen for the accelerating beeps.
3. **Target Outcome:**
   - Every hidden phone is found within 6 minutes.
   - Participants experience firsthand that Bluetooth signals penetrate through walls and can guide them directly to trapped neighbors.
