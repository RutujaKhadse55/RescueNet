# RescueNet Rescuer Field Reference Guide (Phase 16)
## Quick 1-Page Field Card for First Responders (NDRF / SDRF)

---

## 1. Starting Your Shift
1. **Unlock Rescuer Mode:** Open RescueNet $\to$ **Settings** $\to$ **Scan Agency Credential QR**.
2. **Mount Phone:** Keep your phone in an outer chest or shoulder pouch with Bluetooth turned ON.
3. **Check Nearby Clusters:** The home screen displays nearby clusters sorted by distance and medical priority.

---

## 2. Using Homing Mode (Final Approach to Victims)

When you are within 50 meters of an assigned survivor cluster:
1. Tap the cluster card $\to$ tap **"Start Homing Mode"**.
2. **Watch the Trend Guidance (Never look for metric distances):**
   - 🔴 **`COLDER` (Steady Red Bar):** You are walking away from the beacon. Turn 180 degrees.
   - 🟡 **`STEADY` (Yellow Bars):** Signal level is unchanged. Move laterally left or right.
   - 🟢 **`WARMER` (Pulsing Green Bars):** You are approaching the victim! Continue in this direction.
3. **Listen & Feel:**
   - As you get closer, the audio chirp and haptic vibration pulses accelerate (faster beeps = closer).
4. **Physical Obstacle Search:**
   - In rubble or dark smoke, use physical probing in the direction where signal peaked.

---

## 3. Extracting Survivors & Closing Clusters
1. **Upon Physical Contact:**
   - Tap the large green **"Found Them"** button.
   - Enter verified headcount and triage condition.
   - Tap **"Sign & Mark Reached"**.
   - Your phone signs and broadcasts a `REACHED` packet over the mesh. The control room dashboard immediately flips the cluster status to green.
2. **Evacuation Complete:**
   - Once all survivors are safely handed over to medical transport, tap **"Close Incident"**.
   - The victim's phone exits emergency mode and clears high-duty beaconing.

---

## 4. Key Field Safety Rules
* ⚠️ **Never assume the phone is at the exact victim:** Survivors under rubble may have dropped their handset 2–3 meters away. Search the immediate vicinity.
* ⚠️ **Low Battery Warning:** If your phone drops below 20%, replace the battery or switch roles with a teammate to avoid losing recon telemetry.
* ⚠️ **Radio Silence in Flammable Zones:** If combustible gas is suspected, switch handset to Airplane mode before entering confined basement spaces.
